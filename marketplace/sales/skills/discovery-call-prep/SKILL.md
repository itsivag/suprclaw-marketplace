# Discovery Call Prep

Prepare a focused sales conversation that diagnoses the prospect's pain before pitching.

## Output Format

```markdown
# Discovery Call Prep

## Prospect Context
- Lead: ...
- Source: ...
- Known pain/signal: ...
- Unknowns: ...

## Call Objective
- ...

## Questions To Ask
1. ...
2. ...
3. ...

## Qualification Checklist
| Area | What to learn | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Pain | ... | ... |
| Urgency | ... | ... |
| Current workaround | ... | ... |
| Decision process | ... | ... |
| Success criteria | ... | ... |

## Objections To Prepare For
| Likely objection | Response angle |
|---|---|

## Desired Next Step
- ...
```

## Rules

- Start diagnostic, not demo-first.
- Do not claim the prospect has problems not evidenced by context.
- Book or suggest the next meeting only as a recommended action; do not schedule without approval.
